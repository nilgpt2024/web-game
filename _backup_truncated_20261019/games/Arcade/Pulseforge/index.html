How to play

Hit the notes. Four lanes fall toward the bright line. Press the matching key exactly when a note crosses it.
The melody is yours. Drums, bass and pads play on their own — the lead melody only sounds when you hit it. Miss, and the tune goes quiet.
Long notes. Bars with a tail must be held until the end.
Wrong presses cost you. Hitting a lane with nothing in it breaks your combo and shaves a few points — but it never counts against your accuracy, which only ever measures how you timed real notes.
Overdrive. Chaining hits fills the flow meter on the left. Fill it and every point counts double.
Seeds. Any text composes a different song — style, key, tempo, structure and melody. Synthwave, house, trance, drum & bass, chiptune, lo-fi, trap and cinematic each bring their own drums, instruments and mix. Pick one, or let the seed choose. Share the link to share the exact track.
Offset. If your hits feel early or late, run the tap calibration in settings. Headphones beat Bluetooth speakers.

Calibrate

Tap space or the circle on every beat. Twelve taps and we'll work out your offset.
