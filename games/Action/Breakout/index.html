Breakout Game Introduction

Breakout is the 1976 arcade classic that founded the ball-and-paddle genre. Rows of bricks line the top of the screen; you control the paddle at the bottom and bounce the ball up to smash them. Every brick scores points, and clearing the wall advances you to the next level.

The fun lies in catching and aiming: where the ball hits the paddle decides the rebound angle, letting you save rallies or steer the ball into dense brick zones. Fast-paced and instantly playable — an eternal classic.

How to Play Breakout

Move the paddle left and right to catch the falling ball and bounce it into the bricks. Hitting a brick destroys it for points; letting the ball drop below costs a life. Clear all bricks within your lives to advance — the ball speeds up each level.

Controls

  • Mouse move, or Arrow Keys / A D: control the paddle
  • Space: start / serve
  • Mobile: swipe left/right at the bottom
  • Hit with different paddle positions to change the rebound angle

Features

  • Classic paddle-and-ball with rising speed
  • Controllable angles mix reaction and strategy
  • No sign-up — play instantly
  • Clean and smooth on desktop and mobile

Tips & Tricks

  1. Center hits rebound straight; edge hits give sharper angles — use this to rescue or reposition.
  2. Open a channel to the top so the ball can clear bricks from above.
  3. Don't just watch the ball — track its position and move early.
  4. When the ball speeds up, prioritize catching it over aiming.

FAQ

Breakout How do I clear it?

Bounce the ball with your paddle and smash every brick to clear the level.

What happens when the ball drops?

Dropping the ball costs a life; game over when lives run out.

Breakout Is it free?

Completely free — no download or sign-up, play on phone or desktop.

How do I control the ball's direction?

Center hits go straight; edge hits angle sharply — use them to aim at bricks.
