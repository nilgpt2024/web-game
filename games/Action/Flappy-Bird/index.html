Flappy Bird Game Introduction

Flappy Bird is the maddening little game everyone loves to hate: a round bird flies forward past randomly spaced green pipes, and all you can do is tap to flap up and let go to fall.

It looks simple but is brutally hard: narrow gaps and fast gravity punish any hesitation. Pure rhythm control and composure — every mistake restarts from zero, perfect for one more quick run.

How to Play Flappy Bird

Tap (or press Space / click) to flap upward; release and gravity takes over. Fly through the gaps between pipes for 1 point each. Hitting a pipe or the ground ends the run — restart anytime to chase a higher score.

Controls

  • Mouse click / Space: flap upward
  • Mobile: tap the screen to flap
  • Release to let the bird fall
  • Click after game over to restart

Features

  • Simple to learn, hard to put down
  • No ads, no network — runs fully offline
  • Pixel-art bird with a nostalgic feel
  • Perfect for chasing your high score in spare minutes

Tips & Tricks

  1. Tap briefly — holding makes the bird overshoot.
  2. Don't stare at the bird; watch the next gap.
  3. A steady tap rhythm beats frantic clicking through dense pipes.
  4. Mindset matters: restart instantly and focus on the pipe ahead.

FAQ

How do I play Flappy Bird?

Tap to rise, release to fall, score by passing gaps — a pipe hit ends the run.

最高能得多少分？

No cap — your score is simply how many pipes you pass.
