Piano Tiles Game Introduction

Piano Tiles is a rhythm game popular on mobile and web: black tiles fall from the top and you must tap each one before it reaches the bottom line — while never touching the white area.

e tAs speed rises, tap the black tiles in sequence like playing piano. It fuses rhythm with reaction speed, testing finger agility and focus.

How to Play Piano Tiles

he Watch the falling black tiles and tap them as they reach the line. A correct tap plays a piano note; touching white or missing a tile fails. Speed increases as you progress — survive longer for a higher score.
