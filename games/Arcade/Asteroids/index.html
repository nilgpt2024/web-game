How to play

Destroy all asteroids across the wave quota to win. Large rocks split into medium, then small. Avoid collisions and UFO fire.

  • Rotate with ← → or A/D (or on-screen buttons).
  • Thrust with ↑ or W.
  • Fire with Space.
  • Hyperspace with Shift (when available) — random teleport that avoids rocks when possible.
