Asteroids Game Introduction

Asteroids is the 1979 arcade masterpiece whose vector-line style shaped countless space shooters. Pilot your ship through space, rotate to aim and blast the drifting asteroids into pieces.

shiIts signature is real inertia: thrust and your ship keeps gliding, so you must counter-thrust to steer. Big rocks split into smaller, faster shards — tense and addictive.

And addictive.
