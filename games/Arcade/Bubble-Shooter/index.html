Bubble Shooter Game Introduction

Bubble Shooter is the classic casual match game: colorful bubbles fill the top of the screen and you fire matching colors from the bottom to connect three or more and pop them.

Angle and bank shots are key: use wall rebounds to reach tricky spots. As the stack sinks toward the bottom line, keep popping before it touches — planning plus sharp eyes.

How to Play Bubble Shooter

Aim and fire; landing next to two or more same-colored bubbles pops the group, and now-unsupported bubbles drop. Clear the screen to win; touching the danger line ends the game.

Controls

  • Mouse move: aim
  • Left click / Space: fire
  • Mobile: drag to aim, tap to fire
  • Use wall rebounds to adjust landing

Features

  • Classic match-3 bubble play
  • Bank shots add real strategy
  • Chain pops are satisfying
  • No sign-up needed — play right in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Target isolated, same-color clusters for bigger chains.
  2. Bank off walls to reach gaps direct shots can't.
  3. Study the color layout before choosing your shot order.
  4. When the stack nears the line, clear the tallest column first.

FAQ

Bubble ShooterHow do I play it?

Connect three or more same-colored bubbles to pop them; clear the screen to win.

How do I use rebounds?

Bank shots reach spots a straight line can't.

Is the game free?

Completely free — no download or sign-up, play on phone or desktop.

What if bubbles reach the line?

The stack touching the bottom line fails the game — keep popping.
