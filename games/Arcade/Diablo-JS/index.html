How to Play DiabloJS

Goal

Stay alive! Defeat monsters to earn gold and potions, keep your health up and explore the dungeon.

Desktop Controls

Click anywhere to move. Click close to your hero to attack or pick up items. Potions heal you instantly when picked up. Press Tab to toggle the map.

Mobile Controls

Use the virtual joystick (left side) to move. Tap the ⚔️ button to attack or interact, the 🧪 button to drink a potion, and the 🗺️ button to open the map. You can also tap directly on the map to move or interact.

Health

Your health slowly regenerates. Pick up red potions and drink them when your health bar gets low. Monsters will chase and attack you — keep your distance or fight back.

Weapons & Skills

Press Z / X / C (or the 🗾🪃🪄 buttons on mobile) to switch between the fast Blade, the heavy War Axe and the ranged Fire Staff. Cast Q Fireball, W Dash and E WarCry (or the 🔥💨💚 buttons). Skills have cooldowns shown in the HUD.

Levels & Boss

The dungeon has 3 levels: Cellar → Crypt → Demon Lair. Find the glowing stairs to descend, but each level is guarded by a powerful BOSS — slay it to unlock the stairs. Clear the final level to win.

Your Hero

You play the Barbarian — a heavy melee fighter with 2000 HP, three weapons (Blade, War Axe, Fire Staff) and three skills (Fireball, Dash, War Cry). The HUD shows your health, weapon and skill cooldowns.
