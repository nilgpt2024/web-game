DiabloJS — Free Isometric Action RPG

DiabloJS is a classic isometric hack-and-slash action RPG that runs entirely in your browser — no download, no install. Guide your barbarian hero through a dark dungeon, hunt aggressive monsters, grab health potions and gold, and stay alive as long as you can.

  • Touch optimized: virtual joystick + on-screen buttons for mobile play
  • Desktop ready: click-to-move controls with keyboard shortcuts
  • Survival RPG: manage your health, drink potions, defeat monsters for loot
  • Instant play: works on any modern browser
  • Deep combat: switch between Blade / War Axe / Fire Staff, cast Fireball, Dash & Heal, collect potions and buffs
  • 3-level dungeon: descend Cellar → Crypt → Demon Lair and slay each level’s BOSS to win

How to Play DiabloJS

Goal

Stay alive! Defeat monsters to earn gold and potions, keep your health up and explore the dungeon.

Desktop Controls

Click anywhere to move. Click close to your hero to attack or pick up items. Press 1-0 to drink potions from your belt. Press Tab to toggle the map.

Mobile Controls

Use the virtual joystick (left side) to move. Tap the ⚔️ button to attack or interact, the 🧪 button to drink a potion, and the 🗺️ button to open the map. You can also tap directly on the map to move or interact.

Health

Your health slowly regenerates. Pick up red potions and drink them when your health bar gets low. Monsters will chase and attack you — keep your distance or fight back.

Weapons & Skills

Press Z / X / C (or the 🗾🪃🪄 buttons on mobile) to switch between the fast Blade, the heavy War Axe and the ranged Fire Staff. Cast Q Fireball, W Dash and E Heal (or the 🔥💨💚 buttons). Skills have cooldowns shown in the HUD.

Levels & Boss

The dungeon has 3 levels: Cellar → Crypt → Demon Lair. Find the glowing stairs to descend, but each level is guarded by a powerful BOSS — slay it to unlock the stairs. Clear the final level to win.

Choose Your Hero

Pick from 3 classes at the start: the heavy Barbarian, the fast Rogue with ranged bows, or the spell-casting Sorceress. Each has unique weapons, skills, stats and play style — the HUD shows your class and skill cooldowns.
