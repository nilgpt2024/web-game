Doodle Jump Game Introduction

Doodle Jump is the classic vertical jumper: a cute doodle monster bounces endlessly upward, and you tilt (or key) left and right to land on platforms, climb higher and dodge black holes and monsters.

The rhythm is always up: random platform heights, springs and rockets for sudden boosts, plus crumbling and moving platforms for spice. Higher up means wider views but slimmer margins.

How to Play Doodle Jump

Steer left and right; landing on a platform bounces you upward. Springs and rockets launch you higher. Avoid black holes (they suck you in) and monsters (touching them fails). Climb for a new record.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or A / D: move left/right
  • Mobile: tilt the device to steer
  • Springs/rockets launch you upward
  • Dodge black holes and monsters

Features

  • Classic vertical jump gameplay
  • Random platforms keep every run fresh
  • Springs and rockets add variety
  • No sign-up — play right in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Read platform positions early and steer ahead of time.
  2. Veer off black holes immediately — they suck you in.
  3. Landing on a monster's head may bounce it, but side contact fails — keep clear.
  4. Plan the shortest upward path to cut wasted sideways moves.
