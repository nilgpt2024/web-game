How to play

Guide the frog from the bottom grass to empty home slots across the road and river. Classic fixed playfield — not endless.

  • Move: Arrow keys or WASD (one hop per press). Use the on-screen D-pad on mobile.
  • Road: Cars kill on contact.
  • River: Stay on logs or turtles. Water or leaving the screen means death. Riding objects carry you sideways.
  • Homes: Enter an empty slot from the river. Fill the required homes before time runs out.
  • Timer: Resets each life. Time-out costs a life. Zero lives or a failed run ends the game.
  • Hard: Diving turtles, a median snake, and a second faster round after filling all homes.

Frogger Game Introduction

Frogger is the 1981 arcade classic: guide a frog home by crossing a traffic-packed road, then hopping across the river on drifting logs and turtle shells into one of five slots on the far bank.

Two very different halves: dodge speeding cars on the road, then ride the rhythm of logs and turtles on the water. One mistimed hop means water or wheels — pure timing and coordination.

How to Play Frogger

Cross the road through traffic gaps, then hop onto logs or turtles and ride them across. Frogs can't rest on water, and turtles sometimes dive. Fill the five home slots to clear the stage.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or W A S D: move the frog
  • Mobile: swipe or tap on-screen arrows
  • Time your hops to vehicles and logs
  • Water, collisions or timeouts cost lives

Features

  • Road + river two-phase gameplay
  • Logs, turtles and cars as dynamic hazards
  • Faster stages as you advance
  • No sign-up — play instantly

Tips & Tricks

  1. Prefer logs over turtles — turtles dive, so don't linger.
  2. Scope the home slots early and time the final hops.
  3. Pause on the road's median and read the traffic.
  4. Watch the timer — a perfect wait can time you out.
