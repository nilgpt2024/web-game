THE CARPET RIDER’S COMPANION

A little know-how.

01 / Chase the ground

W climb, S descend. A D steer. Low flight is faster and builds skyfire. Hold SHIFT to boost. Skim the glowing cliff faces at 6–46 m altitude for extra speed and skyfire. Keep clear of buildings.

02 / Make a little magic

Aim and hold click to cast. 1 Fireball, 2 Lightning, 3 Wind blast; Q cycles. Wind reflects incoming shots and fans burning enemies. Click an incoming shot just before impact to parry it back at its attacker. Frost sets up fire shatters.

03 / Find your flow

SPACE barrel roll above 4m. Near misses, golden rings and defeats build a chain and refill your Hourglass. Right click toggles slow motion; the meter shows seconds remaining. Archer sightings briefly slow time for 5 reserve. Touch gold wisps to keep your chain alive.

04 / Burn a little brighter

Slay monsters for loot. Spell levels persist through the run; Rapid Fire, Fury, Focus and Overdrive are timed boosts. Bosses fly independently: attack, skim past or boost away. Dune bugs have two fragile wards; other bosses are vulnerable immediately. Pink hearts heal; blue Ward hearts heal and shield. Magnets last 18 seconds, with repeat pickups extending the timer up to 32 seconds.
