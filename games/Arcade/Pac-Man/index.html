Pac-Man Game Introduction

Pac-Man, born in 1980, is one of the most influential arcade games ever — its yellow pie-slice icon is a symbol of gaming itself. Eat every dot in the maze while dodging the roaming colored ghosts.

Power pellets in the corners turn ghosts blue and edible for a short window. That dodge-and-strike rhythm plus each ghost's distinct personality keeps Pac-Man evergreen.

How to Play Pac-Man

Move and eat all dots while dodging the red, pink, blue and orange ghosts. Eat a power pellet to turn them blue briefly and eat them for points. Clear the maze to advance; a ghost catch costs a life.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or W A S D: move
  • Mobile: swipe or tap direction buttons
  • Power pellets let you eat ghosts briefly
  • Eaten ghosts respawn at the center

Features

  • Classic maze play with distinct ghost AI
  • Power pellets flip the tables
  • Dots, ghost chains and fruit score well
  • No sign-up — play right in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Turn early at corners — Pac-Man accelerates slightly, shaking ghosts.
  2. Remember pellet spots and counter-attack when cornered by two or more.
  3. Chain blue ghosts for multiplied points — key to high scores.
  4. Never camp in dead ends; always keep an escape route.
