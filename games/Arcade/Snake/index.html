Snake Game Introduction

Snake is one of the most famous casual games, going global when it shipped on Nokia phones. Steer an ever-moving snake to eat food — each bite grows the body and adds points.

Dead simple rules, but a longer body leaves less room — hit a wall or yourself and it's over. It tests reflexes, foresight and spatial sense in short, fast rounds.

How to Play Snake

Steer up, down, left or right and eat food to grow and score. The snake never stops, can't pause, and can't reverse 180°. Hitting a wall or yourself ends the game — eat more, survive longer.

Controls

  • Arrow keys or W A S D: steer
  • Space: pause / resume
  • Mobile: swipe or tap direction buttons
  • No reversing — turn, then turn again

Features

  • Zero learning curve; a round takes minutes
  • Difficulty grows with the snake
  • High score tracking for replays
  • No sign-up — play in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Move in a snake-like lap early on to save space later.
  2. Plan the route to food before you're close — don't turn at the last second.
  3. With a long body, hug the outside of your own tail to avoid boxing yourself in.
  4. Start turns early, especially at high speed.

FAQ

Snake怎么算赢？

There's no finish line — eat as much as you can and survive as long as possible.

蛇撞到什么会失败？

Touching the wall or your own body ends the game instantly.

游戏Is it free?

Completely free — no download or sign-up; play on mobile or desktop.

为什么不能直接掉头？

A 180° reverse makes the snake hit itself — only 90° turns are allowed.
