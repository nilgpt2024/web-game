Tetris Game Introduction

Tetris, born in the Soviet Union in 1984, is one of the best-selling games ever. Seven tetromino shapes fall from the top; move and rotate them to stack neatly. A fully filled row clears and scores.

Its magic is constant tidying at an ever-faster pace: instant judgments about placement, line clears and room for what's coming next. Simple rules, near-infinite depth.

How to Play Tetris

Move and rotate the falling piece to fit the stack, and soft- or hard-drop it into place. Fill a full row to clear it and score; clearing multiple rows at once (four = a Tetris) scores more. Stack to the top and it's over.

Controls

  • ← →: move · ↑ / X: rotate
  • ↓: soft drop · Space: hard drop
  • C: hold · P: pause
  • Mobile: tap to rotate, swipe to move and drop

Features

  • Classic seven pieces — easy rules, endless depth
  • Hold and next-piece preview for planning
  • Bonus scoring for 2/3/4-line clears
  • No sign-up — play instantly

Tips & Tricks

  1. Keep the stack flat — a single deep well is hard to fill later.
  2. Reserve an edge column as a 4-line well and drop I-pieces for the highest-scoring Tetris.
  3. Check the next piece before placing the current one.
  4. Learn T-spins to wedge T-pieces into gaps — an advanced staple.

FAQ

TetrisHow do I play it?

Move and rotate falling pieces, clear full rows for points — stacking to the top ends the game.

What is a Tetris?

Clearing four rows at once is a Tetris — worth multiples of a single clear, usually with the I-piece.

Is the game free?

Completely free — no download or sign-up; play on mobile or desktop.

What does Hold do?

Hold lets you stash the current piece and swap in a better one — key when shapes don't fit.
