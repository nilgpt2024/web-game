Driving recordings

  • Car Engine Loop — 96 kHz, 4 s by Iwan Gabovitch (qubodup), used under CC BY 3.0. Adapted from the original recording: mono conversion, frequency filtering, level adjustment and a short crossfade at the loop boundary. Playback pitch follows engine load and gear changes.
  • Horn Honk by Mike Koenig, SoundBible, used under CC BY 3.0. Trimmed, filtered, faded and level adjusted. Pitch, distance and stereo position vary in play.
  • Car Tire Squeal / Skid Loop by Tom Haigh (audible-edge), loop prepared by qubodup, used under CC BY 3.0. Resampled, filtered, faded and level adjusted; short excerpts play during harder braking.

These recordings are general vehicle sounds, not recordings of specific Indian vehicle models. Road noise, the suspension thud, the rod swish and metal impact are generated. The optional ORR Drive music is an original electronic composition.

Delivery riders

Stylized Swiggy, Zomato and Blinkit riders wear recognizable orange, red and yellow shirts and carry branded delivery bags.

  • Swiggy: logo and kit reference from the official rider website. Logo asset adapted to white for the orange shirt and bag.
  • Zomato: wordmark from the official website, using its logo asset in white on red. Shirt and bag reference: NDTV Profit photograph.
  • Blinkit: dark and green wordmark from Brandlogos, cropped from the original yellow background without changing its vector shapes. Yellow shirt and bag reference: India Today photograph.

The delivery company names and marks belong to their respective owners. Reference photographs are not included in the game.

Guest scores and privacy

Leaderboard submissions publish your nickname, guest tag, points, finish time and driving statistics. Your browser uses a secure guest cookie to reconnect to the same profile; names do not identify or authorize players. No email address or password is required.

The guest cookie lasts up to one year. Clearing cookies, using private browsing or changing devices can create a new profile; sharing a browser shares its guest profile. Profiles and published personal bests are stored online. Temporary race progress records expire after 24 hours, while published best scores remain.

Scores are checked for plausible route progress, timing and counters before publication. These checks support a casual leaderboard; they do not make a browser-controlled game cheat-proof. Weekly rankings start Monday at 00:00 UTC, using the week in which the attempt began.
