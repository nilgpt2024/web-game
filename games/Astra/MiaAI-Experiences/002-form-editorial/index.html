Dear curious reader,

A circle has no privileged beginning. Turn it and it is still itself. Yet give it weight, cast a shadow, place it beside a square, and suddenly it has a voice.

This issue is an invitation to stay with familiar things long enough for them to become unfamiliar. Good design begins there: in the second look.
