How to play

Classic Battleship on dual 10×10 grids. Place your fleet, then take turns firing one shot at a time (no Salvo).

  • Fleet: Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), Destroyer (2). Ships may touch.
  • Placement: Select a ship from the tray, press Rotate or R to turn, then click your board. Ready starts combat when all ships are placed.
  • Combat: You fire first. Click an enemy cell. Misses mark water; hits mark ships. Sunk ships are announced and revealed on the enemy board.
  • Win: Sink the entire enemy fleet. Lose: your fleet is sunk.
  • AI: Easy fires at random. Medium hunts orthogonally after a hit. Hard continues along aligned hits and uses checkerboard hunting.
  • New Game restarts placement on the same level. Change Level returns to the difficulty screen.

Battleship Game Introduction

Battleship is the classic deduction game: each side secretly places a fleet on a grid sea chart, then they take turns calling coordinates and firing. Hits and sinkings are announced — first to sink the whole enemy fleet wins.

It turns guessing into reasoning: a hit means a ship occupies adjacent cells, so you keep searching around it. Finding the whole fleet on limited shots is pure spatial deduction.

How to Play Battleship

First place your carrier, battleships, cruisers and destroyers on your own chart; then take turns firing at enemy cells. After a hit, keep attacking neighboring cells until the ship sinks.

Controls

  • Drag ships onto your chart as prompted
  • Click enemy cells to fire
  • Hits and misses are clearly marked
  • Sink the entire enemy fleet to win

Features

  • Classic deduction — placement plus hunting
  • Hits lead the hunt with real logic
  • Play vs AI
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. After a hit, probe all four neighbors to find the ship's orientation.
  2. Big ships are easier to hit — spread fire evenly early.
  3. Scatter ships and hug edges to break enemy streaks.
  4. Track confirmed-empty cells so no shot is wasted.
