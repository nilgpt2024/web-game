How to play

Beat the dealer without going over 21. Blackjack pays 3:2. Dealer stands on all 17.

  • Hit — take another card. Stand — keep your hand.
  • Double — double your bet and take exactly one more card (first two cards only).
  • Easy includes a Hint button. Hard starts with fewer chips.
