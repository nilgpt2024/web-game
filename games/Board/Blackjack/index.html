Blackjack Game Introduction

Blackjack is one of the world's most popular casino card games. The goal: get your hand as close to 21 as possible without going over, and beat the dealer. Face cards count 10; an Ace counts 1 or 11.

You decide whether to hit, stand, double, and more. This game uses virtual chips — no real money — ideal for learning the rules and basic strategy.

How to Play Blackjack

Each hand starts with a bet and two cards. Hit for more, Stand to finish; over 21 busts. The dealer typically must draw to 17, then hands compare.

Controls

  • Place your bet with chips
  • Hit: draw another card
  • Stand: end your hand
  • Double and split available in some spots

Features

  • Classic casino rules, easy to learn
  • Hit, stand, double — real decisions
  • Virtual chips — no real money
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. When the dealer shows 2–6, they're likely to bust — stand more conservatively.
  2. On 12–16, your decision hinges on the dealer's upcard.
  3. Double on 11 (or ace-anything soft) in most spots.
  4. Over 21 loses instantly — stand on hard 16 vs a small dealer card.
