How to play

American draughts (checkers) on an 8×8 board. You are Red; the AI is Black.

  • Move: Click a piece, then a highlighted square. Men move forward diagonally; kings move both ways.
  • Capture: Jump an adjacent opponent onto an empty square beyond. Captures are optional — pieces that can jump are highlighted as a hint.
  • Multi-jump: Keep jumping with the same piece when more captures are available.
  • Kings: Reach the opposite end to crown a man. Kings can move and capture in any diagonal direction.
  • Win: Capture all opponent pieces or leave them with no legal moves. Draw if the same position repeats three times.

Checkers Game Introduction

Checkers (Draughts) is an age-old board game: on an 8×8 board, pieces advance diagonally and capture by jumping over the opponent's pieces. Reaching the far rank promotes a piece to a King, which can move backward.

Rules are simple, but forced captures and multi-jumps make it tactical. Win by capturing all enemy pieces or leaving them with no legal move.

How to Play Checkers

On your turn, move a piece one diagonal step forward; if an enemy piece sits diagonally ahead with an empty landing square, you must jump and capture — and can chain jumps. Reach the far rank to promote, allowing backward moves.

Controls

  • Click a piece, then click a target square
  • Captures are usually mandatory
  • Promote at the far rank to a King (moves backward)
  • Win by capturing everything or trapping your rival

Features

  • Simple rules: diagonal moves, jump captures, promotion
  • Forced captures and chains add tactics
  • Play vs AI at multiple difficulties
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. Don't move the back rank early — it blocks enemy promotion.
  2. Set up chain jumps to capture several pieces at once.
  3. Pieces on the edge are safer — one flank can't be attacked.
  4. Sacrifice one piece as bait to set up a multi-capture.
