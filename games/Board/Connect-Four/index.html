Connect Four Game Introduction

Connect Four is the classic vertical-drop game: on a 7x6 board, players take turns dropping discs into a column, where they fall to the lowest open slot. First to line up four horizontally, vertically or diagonally wins.

Rules are minimal but tactics run deep: the center column is prime real estate, and creating double threats wins games. Called baby gomoku, yet even adults blunder in winning positions.

How to Play Connect Four

Pick a column and drop your disc to its lowest open slot. Take turns; first to four in any direction wins. A full board with no four is a draw.

Controls

  • Keys 1-7: choose a column
  • Mobile: tap the column

Tips & Tricks

  1. The center column is most valuable — take it or block it early.
  2. Create double threats: one drop opening two winning lines.
  3. Anticipate the opponent's next move and block developing threes.
  4. Deny even-numbered open slots that hand over key columns.
