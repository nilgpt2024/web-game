Gomoku Game Introduction

五子棋（Gomoku）是起源于中国的经典棋类游戏：黑白双方轮流在 15×15（或 19×19）棋盘上落子，先在横、竖、斜任意方向连成五子（或以上）的一方获胜。

Three minutes to learn, but the depth is remarkable: opening patterns, open threes and fours, forcing moves, and forbidden-move rules. Every expert move balances attack and defense — easy to learn, hard to master.

How to Play Gomoku

Take turns placing stones on intersections, Black first. Five in a row, column or diagonal wins. Against the AI, block its open threes and fours; some rules forbid certain Black moves — check the settings.

Controls

  • Mouse click: place a stone
  • Mobile: tap the board
  • AI or two-player on one screen
  • First to five in a line wins

Features

  • Simple rules — three minutes to learn
  • Endless attack-defense interplay
  • AI battles or local two-player
  • No sign-up — play in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Open in the center — the most four-way potential.
  2. An open three forces a reply — use it to set the pace.
  3. Check the opponent's near-fives even while attacking.
  4. Double-threats like double-threes or broken fours win one way or another.

FAQ

GomokuHow do I win?

Five consecutive stones in any line wins.

Does Black have forbidden moves?

Some rules ban Black's double-threes and double-fours — check in-game settings.

游戏Is it free?

Completely free — no download or sign-up; play on mobile or desktop.

How do I get better?

Learn open threes and fours first, then patterns and forbidden moves — review games with the AI.
