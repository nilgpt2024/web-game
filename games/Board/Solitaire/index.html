Solitaire Game Introduction

Solitaire (the classic Klondike version) is the most iconic single-player card game and a fond memory of idle computer time for millions. The goal is to move every card, by suit from A to K, onto the four foundations in the top-right.

Seven tableau columns fan out below with only the top cards face-up; move cards between columns alternating red and black in descending order, flip face-down cards, and draw from the stock in the top-left. Relaxed-looking, but it demands planning and luck.

How to Play Solitaire

Move cards between the seven columns alternating colors in descending order — a black 7 on a red 8, for example. Send aces to the foundations, then build 2 through K by suit. Collect all four suits from A to K to win.

Controls

  • Drag or click cards to move
  • Click the stock to draw cards
  • Double-click sends a card to a foundation
  • Mobile: tap or drag cards

Features

  • Classic Klondike rules — one game can last a while
  • Draw-one or draw-three modes
  • Auto-complete and hint options
  • No sign-up — play right in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Flip face-down cards early — every reveal adds options.
  2. Don't rush foundations — some cards are more useful left in the tableau.
  3. An empty column only accepts a K (and its run) — use it wisely.
  4. When stuck, draw from the stock and re-check old cards for new moves.

FAQ

Solitaire How do I win?

Build all four suits from A to K on the foundations to win.

How do I move cards between columns?

Cards must alternate red/black in descending order.

Is the game free?

How do draw modes differ?

Draw-one is easier; draw-three is more challenging and memory-heavy.
