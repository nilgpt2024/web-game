Tic-Tac-Toe Game Introduction

Tic-Tac-Toe is the simplest classic pen-and-paper game: on a 3×3 grid, players take turns marking X and O — first to line up three in a row, column or diagonal wins.

A round takes seconds yet holds basic offense and defense. With perfect play it always ends in a draw — a great logic primer and a quick AI match.

How to Play Tic-Tac-Toe

Take turns marking empty cells, one X and one O. Three in any row, column or diagonal wins; a full grid with no line is a draw.

Controls

  • Click an empty cell to mark
  • Touch: tap a cell to mark
  • Play the AI or take turns with a friend
  • Three in a line wins; a full grid draws

Features

  • Simplest rules — 30 seconds a round
  • AI or two-player modes
  • Trains attack, defense and spatial logic
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. Open at center — it's part of four possible lines.
  2. If center is taken, grab a corner — three line chances each.
  3. Two simultaneous threats are unstoppable — block the first one early.
  4. As second player, correct blocks guarantee no loss.
