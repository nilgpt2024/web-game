ACT I · THE CELESTIAL ROTUNDA

WHERE BRONZE MEETS LIVING BOTANY

Suspended above the clouds of Olympos floats the Verdant Sanctum—an eternal temple where ancient Greek stonework and clockwork mechanisms breathe with solar mana. Here, the Aegis shield guards the heart of botanical creation against the clockwork automata legions.

ACT II · TACTICAL MAZING FREEDOM

SCULPT THE STONE.
DICTATE THE PATH.

No guided lanes. No predetermined corridors. Across a thirty-six by twenty floating marble island, you dictate where the automata march. Every tower erected shifts the real-time pathing, turning a direct dash into an agonizing detour through fields of botanical fire.

ACT III · SACRED FIREPOWER

WEAPONS OF PETAL & PRISM

Mount four archetypes of botanical artillery onto your marble spires: rapid-spin Petal Gatlings with hydraulic recoil, arcing Bloom Mortars that crater the earth with blast waves, crystalline Prism Pillars channeling ramping solar rays, and harmonic Resonance Obelisks that shatter enemy armor.

ACT IV · THE COLD STEEL LEGION

THE GOLIATH AWAKENS

From the smoldering horizon comes the Clockwork Legion. Agile Skitter Scouts dart in swarms, heavy Tread Tanks deflect kinetic shrapnel with cast-iron armor, Rotor Drones bypass ground corridors entirely, and decennial Goliath Colossi advance with devastating siege power.

ACT V · RELICS & SORCERY

HARVEST SCRAP.
INVOKE SOLAR FLARES.

Defeated automata yield sacred scrap. Reinvest into run-to-run Verdant Glyphs to permanently amplify foundry damage, expand starting treasuries, and fortify the Sanctum's living root structure. When swarms threaten to breach, channel orbital Solar Flares and root entire armies in seconds.
