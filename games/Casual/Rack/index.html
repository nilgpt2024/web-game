How to play

Make words crossword-style from your seven tiles. Every word goes in one row or one column and has to touch the tiles already down; the first one covers the star.

Coloured squares multiply what lands on them for the first time: 2L/3L a letter, 2W/3W the whole word. Every new word you form scores, including the short ones across. Using all seven tiles at once is worth 50 extra. Blanks can be any letter and score nothing.

Placing tiles: drag them, or tap a tile then a square. Tap a tile you've put down to take it back. With a keyboard, click a square and type; click it again to turn the arrow, Backspace takes back, Enter plays.

The game ends when the bag is empty and someone plays out, or after six scoreless turns in a row. Letters left on a rack count against you.

After each of your moves the log shows the best play you had. The referee is word-list (MIT), about 270,000 words; the easier computers draw on SCOWL's everyday tiers. Both load once from a CDN.
