Nazi Zombies Portable Game Introduction

纳粹僵尸（Nazi Zombies Portable）是一款第一人称射击打僵尸游戏，灵感来自经典合作生存玩法。你手持武器站在被僵尸包围的战场上，僵尸会一波接一波从四周涌来，你需要一边瞄准射击、一边移动躲避，在弹药耗尽前尽可能活得久。

The core is survival pressure management: later waves bring more and faster zombies, demanding better movement and ammo planning. FPS shooting meets tower-defense wave pressure — tight, tense and built for zombie fans chasing survival records.

How to Play Nazi Zombies Portable

Use the mouse to look, WASD to move, and fire at zombies. Clear each wave to advance — difficulty rises. Watch your ammo and aim for headshots for extra damage. Survive more waves for a higher score until the zombies take you down.

Controls

  • Mouse: look and aim
  • W A S D: move
  • Left click: shoot
  • R: reload; Shift: sprint

Features

  • First-person zombie action, immersive
  • Wave survival — the tension builds
  • Tests movement, aim and ammo management
  • No download or sign-up — play in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Go for headshots — more damage per bullet.
  2. Never stand and spray — kite backward while firing.
  3. Reload between waves, not when zombies are on you.
  4. Listen for footsteps and ambience to predict zombie directions.

FAQ

Nazi Zombies How do I play it?

Aim with the mouse, move with WASD, and survive wave after wave for the highest score.

Does it support gamepads?

This version is keyboard-and-mouse first, with mobile touch controls where noted on the page.

Is the game free?

完全免费，无需下载注册，手机和电脑play in the browser。

Do zombies get stronger?

Yes — later waves bring more, faster zombies that demand better movement and ammo discipline.
