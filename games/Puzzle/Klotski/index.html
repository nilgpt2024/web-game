Klotski Game Introduction

Klotski is the classic Chinese sliding-block puzzle, ranked with the Rubik's Cube among the world's great brain-teasers. Blocks of various sizes crowd the board; slide them to free a path for the biggest block (usually Cao Cao) to the exit.

You can't lift or jump blocks — only slide within tight space, demanding spatial thinking and patience. The classic Heng Dao Li Ma layout alone needs dozens of optimal moves.

How to Play Klotski

Slide blocks around the board to clear the way, then move the 2x2 master block out through the bottom exit. Blocks only translate into empty space — no rotating or overlapping.

Controls

  • Drag with mouse, or click then use arrow keys
  • Mobile: hold and slide with your finger
  • Reset: restore the layout
  • Undo: step back

Features

  • Multiple classic layouts, rising difficulty
  • Tracks moves and time for optimal-solution hunting
  • Undo and hints keep you unstuck
  • Fully offline — play instantly

Tips & Tricks

  1. Always clear the master block's path — no aimless shuffling.
  2. Coordinate vertical pawns — they usually create the needed space.
  3. Before each step down, plan how to clear the horizontal blocks below.
  4. Memorize optimal move counts as personal challenge targets.
