Lights Out Game Introduction

Lights Out is a math puzzle: pressing a cell toggles it and its four orthogonal neighbors. The goal is to turn every light off with as few presses as possible.

Behind the random-looking presses lies linear algebra, and boards usually have a systematic solution. With the right method, even hard layouts reduce step by step to all-off.

How to Play Lights Out

Click a cell to toggle it and its four neighbors. Use row-by-row clearing: for each lit cell, press the cell directly below it, working top to bottom.

Controls

  • Click a cell to toggle lights
  • Touch: tap a cell
  • The clicked cell and its cross-neighbors flip together
  • Clear when every light is off

Features

  • One press flips a cross of five cells
  • Mathematically solvable row by row
  • Many levels and starting layouts
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. Clear rows by pressing directly below each lit cell — top row to bottom.
  2. The first row's presses determine everything below.
  3. Never press the same cell twice — it cancels out.
  4. If the last row won't clear, change the first row's presses and retry.
