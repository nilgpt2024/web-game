Minesweeper Game Introduction

Minesweeper is the classic logic deduction game that shipped with Windows for generations. Every opened cell shows a number — the count of mines in its 8 neighbors. Deduce which cells are safe and which hide mines.

It looks like luck but is pure deduction: numbers interlock, and almost every board resolves safely through logic. Flag confirmed mines and open every safe cell to win.

How to Play Minesweeper

Left-click to open a cell; right-click (or long-press) to flag suspected mines. Numbers show mine counts in the surrounding 8 cells. Deduce, flag, and open every safe cell — one mine click and it's over.

Controls

  • Left click: open a cell
  • Right click: flag / unflag
  • Touch: tap to open, long-press to flag
  • Double-click (or both buttons) on a number to chord-open neighbors when flags match

Features

  • Beginner to expert — bigger boards, more mines
  • Pure logic — patterns reveal themselves with practice
  • No sign-up — play in the browser
  • Builds focus, deduction and patience

Tips & Tricks

  1. The first click is almost always safe — start center or corner.
  2. A 1 with a single unopened neighbor means that cell is a mine.
  3. Subtract adjacent numbers — the difference often pins down mines.
  4. When unsure, process all certain areas before guessing.
