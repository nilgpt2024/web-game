How to play

Nonogram (Picross) is a logic puzzle. Numbers on each row and column show consecutive blocks of filled cells. Solve the picture by filling the right cells.

  • Left-click / tap — toggle empty ↔ fill. Clears a mark first.
  • Right-click / long-press — toggle empty ↔ X mark (known empty).
  • Marks are notes only; they do not count toward the solution.
  • You win when every filled cell matches the solution (extras fail).
  • Easy includes Hint: reveals one correct unfilled cell.
  • Desktop & mobile supported. Long-press ≥400ms to mark on touch.

Nonogram Game Introduction

Nonogram (also called Paint by Numbers) is a number puzzle: each row and column carries a clue of numbers describing its consecutive filled cells. Deduce which cells to fill to reveal a hidden pixel picture.

Like Sudoku it's pure logic: a clue of "1 2" means one block of 1 and one block of 2, in order, with at least one empty between. Clues interlock — every deduction unlocks more, with great payoff.

How to Play Nonogram

Using the row and column clues, click cells to fill or mark them empty. Numbers give the length and order of consecutive blocks, each separated by at least one blank. Fill every cell correctly to complete the picture.

Controls

  • Mouse click: fill / unfill a cell
  • Right-click or mark key: flag empty cells
  • Mobile: tap to fill, long-press to mark
  • Done when every cell is correct

Features

  • Pure logic — no guessing
  • Pictures from simple to complex
  • Builds logic and range reasoning
  • No sign-up — play in the browser

Tips & Tricks

  1. Start with full-length clues — fill those rows and columns at once.
  2. Marking blanks matters as much as filling — empty info drives deduction.
  3. For large numbers, lock the only possible range first, then narrow it.
  4. Stuck? Return to row-column intersections — two clues together usually resolve instantly.
