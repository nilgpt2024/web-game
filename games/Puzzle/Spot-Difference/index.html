Spot the Difference Game Introduction

Spot the Difference is the classic observation game: two seemingly identical images hide several subtle differences — in color, shape, count or position — that you must find within the time limit.

Clicking a difference marks it with a circle. It tests eyesight, patience and focus — rich visuals, fun for all ages, great for relaxing.

How to Play Spot the Difference

Compare both images closely and click each difference you find to mark it. Find them all (usually 5) to pass; careless clicks may hint or penalize.

Controls

  • Click the difference on either image
  • Touch: tap the difference
  • Correct spots get circled
  • Hints reveal one spot if available

Features

  • Classic eye-spy fun for all ages
  • Rich scenes and many levels
  • Builds observation, focus and patience
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. Scan region by region across both images — better than a whole-picture sweep.
  2. Focus on edges, counts and color shades — differences hide there.
  3. Flick your gaze between images; mismatches pop out.
  4. Find the obvious ones first, then hunt the subtle.
