Tangram Game Introduction

Tangram is the classic Chinese dissection puzzle: five triangles, one square and one parallelogram. Just seven pieces, yet they form thousands of figures — people, animals, buildings and more.

You're given a silhouette and must drag and rotate all seven pieces to fill it perfectly. Superb training for spatial imagination and geometric intuition.

How to Play Tangram

Study the silhouette, drag the seven pieces inside, and rotate (or flip) so edges and corners align until the outline is fully covered.

Controls

  • Drag pieces to move them
  • Rotate with buttons or drag
  • Some versions support flipping
  • Done when all seven fill the silhouette

Features

  • Chinese classic — seven pieces, endless shapes
  • Builds spatial and geometric skills
  • Many levels and target figures
  • Runs fully offline — no sign-up

Tips & Tricks

  1. Place the two big triangles first — they set the structure.
  2. Fit the parallelogram and square against straight edges and right angles.
  3. When stuck, try every piece at every angle before moving on.
  4. Watch for 45-degree diagonals in the silhouette — they match triangle edges.
