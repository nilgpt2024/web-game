How to play

Guess the hidden word in a limited number of tries.

Each guess must be a valid word. Type with the keyboard or tap the on-screen keys, then press Enter.

After each guess, the tiles show:

  • W Green — letter is in the word and in the correct spot.
  • E Yellow — letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.
  • A Gray — letter is not in the word.

Choose a level: Easy (4 letters), Medium (5), Hard (6), or Expert (7). Turn on Show hints for a small clue about the word.

Wordle Game Introduction

Wordle is the phenomenon daily word game: six tries to guess a five-letter English word. After each guess, tile colors tell you how close you are — green for correct letter and spot, yellow for right letter wrong spot, gray for not in the answer.

One puzzle per day, and the simple rules plus one-more-try tension conquered the world. It's entertainment, vocabulary practice and deduction training all at once.

How to Play Wordle

Submit a five-letter word, then narrow down with the colors: keep greens fixed, relocate yellows, drop grays. Guess the word within six tries to win.

Controls

  • Type five letters and press Enter
  • Backspace deletes a letter
  • Mobile: tap the on-screen keyboard
  • Use color clues to eliminate letters and spots

Features

  • A fresh puzzle daily — a light daily habit
  • Intuitive color feedback, step-by-step deduction
  • Playable offline — open and go
  • Builds English vocabulary and spelling

Tips & Tricks

  1. Open with a word rich in common vowels like A, E, O with no repeats — maximum information.
  2. Re-try yellow letters in new spots, and watch for duplicates in the answer.
  3. Track gray letters so you never reuse them.
  4. Later guesses should test several candidate letters at once.

FAQ

What do the Wordle colors mean?

Green = right letter, right spot; yellow = right letter, wrong spot; gray = not in the word.

How many times a day can I play?

One puzzle per day — solve it or use six tries, then wait for tomorrow's word.

Is Wordle free?

Completely free — no download or sign-up; play in your browser.

英语不好Can I play it?

Absolutely — use the color clues and common five-letter words, and pick up vocabulary along the way.
