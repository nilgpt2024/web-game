Number Merge Game Introduction

Number Merge is a light number puzzle in the 2048 family: equal tiles collide and merge into bigger numbers for higher scores.

Mechanics vary — tap adjacent equal numbers, slide the board, or link chains. Simple rules and a light pace that also train number sense and planning.
