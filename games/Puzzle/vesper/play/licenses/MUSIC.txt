Ember Variations - original Vesper score (Phase 16, recipe 1)

Ten original compositions and additive-synthesis WAVs created locally for Vesper
with scripts/generate-music.mjs. No third-party music, samples, audio model,
paid service or recorded performance is used in these files.

The Vesper project owner may use, modify and distribute these generated assets
with the project. No third-party attribution or royalty obligation is introduced.
The reproducible recipe, note counts, parameters and SHA256 hashes are recorded
in docs/asset-provenance/phase16-music.json. The UI identifies the score here.

The previous Hiraeth MP3 by Scott Buckley is no longer shipped or played.
