About suipce.com

The name covers two separate codebases that happen to share a domain family. The tools site is a set of single-purpose utilities that do one job and get out of the way. The games site is a curated shelf: games are selected, checked for actually being playable, and annotated with the details of how they were generated.

Keeping them apart is deliberate. A page that tries to be a converter, a calculator and an arcade at once ends up serving none of them well, and search engines treat it the same way. So tools stay at tools.suipce.com, games stay at game.suipce.com, and this page only does one thing: send you to the right one.

Everything here is built and maintained by one person, with AI doing a large share of the drafting. If a page is broken or a game will not load, the contact link at the bottom reaches a human.
